# Introduction

An invitation into The Guide: why it exists, the posture that shapes it, and a path for beginning the journey.

## Begin Here

If you are new to The Guide, begin with its purpose and heart. These documents explain that the Guide is a companion to Scripture, not a replacement for it. They invite us to approach God's Word with humility and attentiveness.

## What You'll Find Here

- [Purpose of the Guide](./purpose-of-the-guide.md) — Why this living companion exists.
- [Heart of the Guide](./heart-of-the-guide.md) — The humble posture that shapes every page.
- [Philosophy of the Guide](./philosophy-of-the-guide.md) — The convictions that keep Scripture central.
- [Formation Compass](./formation-compass.md) — An orientation to the rhythm of Scripture, prayer, reflection, remembrance, and walking.
- [Path Into the Guide](./path-into-the-guide.md) — A suggested way to enter and explore the Guide.

## Where to Go Next

When you are ready to begin, follow the [Reading Path](../reading-path/) into Scripture or explore the practices throughout the Guide.
