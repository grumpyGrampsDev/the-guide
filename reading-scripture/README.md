# Reading Scripture

Learning to approach God's Word carefully, faithfully, and in its intended context before asking how it applies to our lives.

## A Way of Reading

These resources move from a humble approach to Scripture toward careful observation, faithful interpretation, and obedient response. The aim is not simply to collect information or reach quick conclusions, but to listen carefully to what God has communicated.

## What You'll Find Here

- [How to Approach Scripture](./how-to-approach-scripture.md) — Beginning with humility and attentiveness.
- [Read Slowly](./read-slowly.md) — Making room to understand, believe, and live God's Word.
- [Learn to Notice](./learn-to-notice.md) — Attending to what the text actually says.
- [Ask Good Questions](./ask-good-questions.md) — Letting questions come before conclusions.
- [Understand Author Intent](./understand-author-intent.md) — Reading passages in light of what their authors intended to communicate.
- [Interpret Faithfully](./interpret-faithfully.md) — Seeking to understand Scripture in its context and in light of Scripture.
- [Apply Faithfully](./apply-faithfully.md) — Responding in a way that grows from truth rather than replacing it.

## How to Use This Shelf

Move slowly and return to the passage itself as you read. Let observation come before interpretation, and interpretation before application. When a question remains, allow yourself to sit with it. reflect on it.
