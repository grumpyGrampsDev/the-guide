# Formation

Exploring the connected rhythm of Scripture, prayer, reflection, remembrance, and walking as we learn to return to God again and again.

## The Formation Rhythm

The Guide is shaped by this rhythm:

Scripture → Prayer → Reflection → Remembrance → Walking

These practices are not a system for spiritual achievement or a measure of maturity. They name a pattern that emerged through the repeated practice of listening to God's Word, responding to Him, considering His truth, remembering His faithfulness, and taking the next faithful step.
