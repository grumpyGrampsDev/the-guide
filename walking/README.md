# Walking

Considering how to respond to God's Word with faithful obedience, one faithful step at a time.

## What This Shelf Holds

Walking is not self-directed striving. It is trusting God's guidance, depending on His grace, and seeking to obey Him in the present place He has given us.

## What You'll Find Here

- [Purpose of Walking](./purpose-of-walking.md) — Why faithful response belongs to the life of faith.
- [Walking in the Light](./walking-in-the-light.md) — Living by the light God has given.
- [The Next Faithful Step](./next-faithful-step.md) — Moving forward without waiting for perfect understanding.

## A Gentle Invitation

The path ahead may not always be clear. Begin with the light God has given through His Word, pray honestly, and take the next faithful step. Faithfulness, not perfection, is the aim.
