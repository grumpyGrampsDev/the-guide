# The Library

The Library gathers every book within The Guide into one place.

Some shelves explore how to approach Scripture. Others focus on prayer, reflection, remembrance, or faithful walking. Some contain examples and field notes from the journey itself. You are welcome to browse, linger, and return whenever a particular season calls for it. There is no requirement to read every book or to read them in a particular order.

The Guide offers a recommended path for those who are just beginning, but it is also meant to be explored. Follow your curiosity. Revisit familiar shelves. Allow Scripture to remain the foundation while these books simply point you back toward it.

## Browse the Shelves

Each shelf introduces a different part of the journey.

Some establish the posture of The Guide. Some teach practices that have emerged while reading God's Word. Others preserve reminders of God's faithfulness or examples of what these rhythms can look like in everyday life. Every shelf begins with its own introduction before inviting you into the books it contains.

## A Suggested Path

If this is your first visit, consider beginning with the **Introduction** shelf before continuing into the **Reading Path** and **Reading Scripture** shelves.

After that, explore freely. Return often. Read slowly.

And above all, keep opening your Bible.
