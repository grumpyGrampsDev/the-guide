# Field Notes

Reflections, examples, and observations from the journey that point beyond personal experience toward God's ongoing faithfulness.

## What This Shelf Holds

Field Notes gathers lived examples of the practices described throughout The Guide, reflections on Scripture, and resources that have shaped the journey. These notes are not offered as a model others must imitate or as a substitute for personal study. They are testimonies of questions and reminders encountered along the way, shared in the hope that they encourage a return to God's Word.

## What You'll Find Here

- [A Day With the Guide](./a-day-with-the-guide.md) — An example of the Guide's practices in an ordinary day.
- [Hebrews 6 Reflection Example](./hebrews-6-reflection-example.md) — A reflection shaped by careful attention to Scripture.
- [Scripture That Shaped the Guide](./scripture-that-shaped-the-guide.md) — Passages that have helped form this journey.
- [Resources That Shaped the Journey](./resources-that-shaped-the-journey.md) — Companions and influences received with Scripture remaining the authority.

## How to Read the Notes

Read them as companions, not conclusions. Notice the practices at work, test every observation against Scripture, and allow the examples to send you back to your own Bible, prayer, reflection, and faithful walking.
