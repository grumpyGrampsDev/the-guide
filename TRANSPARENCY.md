# Transparency

Every generation receives new tools.

Pens became pencils.

Pencils became typewriters.

Typewriters became word processors.

Today, we also have software capable of assisting with organization, editing, and language.

The Guide has been developed with the assistance of modern software tools, including large language models (LLMs).

That statement deserves some explanation.

This repository began long before it became a collection of Markdown files. It grew from reading Scripture, asking questions, praying, journaling, and reflecting on what God has been teaching me through His Word. As those notes accumulated, modern software became a valuable aid in organizing ideas, refining language, improving clarity, identifying inconsistencies, and strengthening the overall structure of the project.

The use of these tools does not change the purpose of this repository.

The Guide is not intended to produce new revelation, uncover hidden meanings within Scripture, or replace careful study. It exists to encourage readers to slow down, open their Bibles, ask good questions, pray honestly, reflect thoughtfully, and walk faithfully.

Every suggestion produced by software has been treated as a suggestion.

Like insights gained from conversations, sermons, study Bibles, commentaries, or other resources, every idea has been weighed against Scripture rather than accepted automatically.

The responsibility for what appears in this repository remains my own. The content has been reviewed, considered, and intentionally included as part of the ongoing stewardship of this project.

The tools used to develop this repository may change over time, but the purpose of the project remains the same.

Scripture alone is the authority.

If anything written here faithfully reflects God's Word, give thanks to God.

If anything written here fails to do so, reject it.

This guide remains subject to the same standard it encourages every reader to adopt:

Test everything against Scripture.

Retain what is true.

Reject what is not.

Technology can help us communicate more clearly.

It cannot replace the work of carefully reading God's Word, the wisdom of the local church, the guidance of the Holy Spirit, or the lifelong process of being formed into the likeness of Christ.

My hope is simply that these tools have helped remove unnecessary obstacles between the reader and the purpose of this project:

Don't panic.

Open your Bible.
