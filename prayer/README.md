# Prayer

Learning to respond honestly to the God who has revealed Himself through Scripture with dependence, gratitude, confession, questions, and trust.

## What This Shelf Holds

Prayer is not merely a list of requests or a practice to perform correctly. It is relationship: bringing ourselves truthfully before God and responding to what He has revealed.

## What You'll Find Here

- [Purpose of Prayer](./purpose-of-prayer.md) — Why prayer belongs at the center of a life responsive to God.
- [Praying Honestly](./praying-honestly.md) — Bringing gratitude, confession, questions, needs, worship, and trust before God.
- [Daily Prayer Rhythm](./daily-prayer-rhythm.md) — A recurring practice for returning to God through ordinary days.
- [Prayer Set](./prayer-set.md) — A collection for supporting continued prayer.

## How to Use This Shelf

Come without pretending. Let Scripture shape your prayers, and bring what is true before God as it arises. The aim is not perfect words, but continued dependence on Him.
