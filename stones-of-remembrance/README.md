# Stones of Remembrance

Remembering the truths and lessons through which God has shown His faithfulness, so that we can return to them with gratitude and trust.

## What Is a Stone?

A Stone of Remembrance is not a record of our accomplishment or something made for the sake of collecting. It is a truth God has used to shape us over time: a reminder of His character, a lesson from Scripture, a prayer answered, a moment of conviction, or a season in which He sustained us.

Stones emerge. They are markers that point beyond us and back to God's faithfulness.
